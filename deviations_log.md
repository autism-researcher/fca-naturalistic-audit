| 2026-09-19 | **Wording correction, feature-bound sensitivity check.** The supplementary material described the $\pm10\%$ feature-bound perturbation (`scripts/07_sensitivity.py`, Section S.VI) as "exactly equivalent to a $\pm10\%$ physical-bound perturbation" for the six upper-bound-normalized features.
